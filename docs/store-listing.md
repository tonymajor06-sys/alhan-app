**Description** [4000]

Alhan brings the hymns and responses of the Coptic Orthodox Church to your phone, in Coptic, English and Arabic.

EVERY HYMN IN FIVE WAYS
Read each hymn in Coptic, Coptic in English letters, English, Arabic in English letters, or Arabic. Put two side by side, verse by verse, to follow the meaning as you chant.

THE MIDNIGHT PRAISES (TASBEHA)
The whole order of the Midnight Praises: the four Canticles with their Lobsh and Psalis, the Commemoration, the Doxologies, and the Theotokia for every day of the week.

DEACON & ALTAR RESPONSES
The responses between the priest, deacons and people for Vespers, Matins, the Offering of the Lamb and the Liturgy of the Word, with each speaker shown in their own colour.

LEARN THE HYMNS
Listen to recordings, slow them down without changing the pitch, and repeat one verse until you know it. The verse being sung lights up as the audio plays. Ideal for deacons' schools, choirs and Sunday school.

PROJECTOR MODE
Show any hymn on a church screen, one verse at a time, with Coptic, English and Arabic together in large, clear text.

THE COPTIC CALENDAR
Today's Coptic date, the current season and what's coming next, with home-screen and Lock Screen widgets. Turn on reminders to hear the evening before each feast and fast, then tap to open its hymns.

MADE FOR CHURCH
• Search titles and words in every language, in Coptic letters or English letters
• Share any hymn with a link
• Build a playlist and download recordings for offline listening
• Adjustable text size, and the screen stays on while you read
• Dark and light themes
• Full Arabic interface

Free, with no ads and no account.

**What's new** [4000]
First release: hymns, Midnight Praises, deacon responses, audio with learning mode, projector mode, the Coptic calendar with widgets, and feast reminders.
